## YouTube description

An agency agrees to build a website. Halfway through, the client asks for Spanish-language pages. The launch date and budget still matter. What should change?

Pactshift turns that conversation into three clear choices: add budget, exchange planned work, or save the request for later. When the client decides, the project agreement updates with them.

In this demo, a 12-hour language addition replaces a planned 16-hour resource library. The website stays at $12,000, the delivery date stays the same, and total estimated scope moves from 96 to 92 hours. Both sides get a clear record of the decision.

Try the interactive demo: https://pactshift.web.app
Explore the code: https://github.com/shi1720/Galuxium-Nexus-V2
Testing guide: https://github.com/shi1720/Galuxium-Nexus-V2/blob/main/docs/TESTING_INSTRUCTIONS.md

Built for small web and design agencies by Shivam Gupta, founder and project owner, with substantial AI assistance for development, research, testing support, and documentation. Narration in this video is AI-generated.

The product uses React, TypeScript, Express, Firebase Hosting, Google Cloud Run, Firestore, and optional Vertex AI analysis. The agency reviews estimates and choices; application rules control prices and accepted changes.

This demonstration uses fictional project data. A recorded client decision is not proof of payment or independently verified identity. Pricing is proposed, paid checkout is not active, and no customers or revenue are claimed.
